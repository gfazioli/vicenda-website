# Vicenda

**Your mail is not a list.**

Every mail client's pitch is that it sorts better than the last one. Vicenda's
difference sits upstream of sorting: it stops treating your mail as a list at
all.

A receipt, a security advisory and a colleague waiting on an answer are three
different kinds of thing. A list shows them in the same column, in the same
font, at the same weight, and leaves the separating to you. Vicenda separates
them first:

- **Machines get channels.** A sender that writes to you every day about
  deployments is a place you visit, like a channel in a chat app. You scan it
  when you want to, and it does not follow you around.
- **People get threads.** A person waiting on an answer is a debt you owe.
  Those belong in a short list that empties, not in a stream that never does.
- **Recognised machine mail becomes a card.** An invoice has an amount, a card,
  an item and an order number — so Vicenda binds those and draws them, instead
  of rendering somebody's HTML and hoping.

<Callout type="info">
  Vicenda is in a **closed beta**. There is no public download: the disk image
  is sent by hand to people who ask. [Ask for an invite](/beta).
</Callout>

## What it is for

It was built for one specific problem: seven mailboxes across Gmail, a PEC and
a work account, and no client that could answer four questions at a glance.

1. **Which of the accounts did this land in?** Colour answers it — one hue per
   mailbox, and nothing else in the app is allowed to spend colour.
2. **What needs a reply, as against what is bulk?** The two populations are
   separated at the door, not filtered afterwards.
3. **How do I stay fast across all of them?** Everything is local. There is no
   round trip to a server to open a message.
4. **How do I keep one taxonomy across providers?** Labels come through as they
   are, with the colours you gave them.

## What it is not

It is not a product, and this page is not a launch. There is no pricing, no
telemetry, no App Store listing and no roadmap that anybody is owed. It is a
**reading and triage tool first** — composing is secondary, and sending is not
built yet.

That shapes the beta as much as the app: it is closed because the honest size
of this is small.
